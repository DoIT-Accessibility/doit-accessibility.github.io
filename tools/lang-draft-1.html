Use the Translate app

  1. Tap Translate in the Dock.
  2. Choose English and the customer's language.
  3. Tap Conversation, then the layout button and Face to Face.
  4. Take turns speaking. Keep sentences short.

For Spanish and Polish, you can also use the Phrasebook.

High-stakes matter, or the translation looks wrong? Get an interpreter.

Get an interpreter

Un intérprete gratuito está en camino. · Łączymy z bezpłatnym tłumaczem.

  1. Open Propio ONE in the Dock, or open Propio ONE on the web.
  2. Choose the language. For ASL, choose American Sign Language.
  3. Tap Video for ASL, or Audio for a spoken language.
  4. Turn the iPad so the customer and the interpreter can see each other.

If the video freezes, or the customer asks, offer to schedule an in-person interpreter.

Shortcuts

  • Triple-click the top button for Live Speech, Live Captions or Guided Access.
  • Translate stays in On-device mode. Don't turn it off.

Before the next customer

  1. Tap Clear in Type to Talk. Text also clears after 2 minutes idle.
  2. Clear Translate history if anything personal was said.
  3. Wipe the screen.
