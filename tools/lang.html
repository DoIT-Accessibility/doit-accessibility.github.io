Live Captions

X
  • Overview

    Live Captions shows the text of what you speak into the microphone.

    It’s not perfect so use it for quick conversations.

    For more important information, use Type to Talk or Propio One.

  • Show Captions

    Go to Settings > Accessibility > Hearing > Live Captions

    Switch Live Captions on

    Double tap to expand or shrink

  • Customize Appearance

    Go to Settings > Accessibility > Hearing > Live Captions

    Tap Appearance

    Set Text Size, Font, and Colors

  • Type to Talk

    You can also use the Type to Talk app for captions

    Tap in the typing area to bring up the keyboard

    Tap the microphone button to dictate
